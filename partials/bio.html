Short Bio

Quang-Thinh Bui is a senior lecturer at Tien Giang University, Vietnam, where he currently serves as the Deputy Head in charge of the Office of Scientific Research, Technology Management, and International Cooperation and leads the Applied Mathematics and Data Science (AMaDaS) Research Group. He received the B.S. degree in Mathematics and the M.S. degree in Geometry and Topology from Ho Chi Minh City University of Education, Vietnam, and the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from VSB–Technical University of Ostrava, Czech Republic, in 2022. His research focuses on applied topology, fuzzy theory, soft computing, data mining, and representation learning, particularly on developing mathematical and computational approaches for analyzing high-dimensional, incomplete, and noisy data. His work integrates topological data analysis with machine learning and fuzzy systems to uncover structural patterns in complex datasets. Dr. Bui has published research in reputable journals and conferences, including IEEE TNNLS, IEEE TFS, INS, KBS, ESWA, EAAI, WIREs DMKD, and JAMC, as well as international conferences such as ACIIDS and ICCCI.
